"hwado 2.0"



hwado is a Korean term symbolysing
the entire journey of creativity and imagination
through an artistic realisation,
and goes beyond the act of creating.

hwado comes from the concept of the way with 道 “do” meaning path
and the concept of art expression with 畫 “hwa” : painting.
it refers to a continuous cycle of creation rather than a simple result,
like a way of life rather than a passing interest.
it is a discipline that nourishes me,
a way of practising that motivates me.

each image i bring back from my treks reflects the light, colours
and textures i encounter in the field.
these photographs are not ends in themselves, but a reminder that
lived experiences become artistic material :
starting point for post-prod. works mixing digital alterations
with the use of other media,
carried out upon my return to the studio.
the work does not arise from a single moment,
but from a repetitive cycle of walking, perception,
memory, recomposition and contemplation.

without forgetting the memory of a place.


my work explores the transformation of the mountain experiences
into a virtuous cycle of digital, photographic and visual art creations.
working on the boundary between lived experience and its representations,
without forgetting on the tension encountered during isolation in the mountains
and upon returning to everyday life in the studio.

hwado 2.0 takes this concept further
by moving beyond the strict limits of the artistic process.
it is no longer simply a matter of expressing a creative journey,
but of integrating this movement as a way of living my art.
this evolution reflects the acceptance of this cycle as a lasting balance,
where practice, experience and everyday life are no longer separate,
but form a single, continuous space.
hwado 2.0 becomes a way of filling the time between my life and the mountains.


the days of walking,
the hardships of hiking,
the places photographed, where no one goes,
the memory of touching the rocks,
the smells and the people encountered,
the feelings experienced,
the return to the studio,
the post production processes,
the daily mental struggles,
the cycle of routine that begins again,
again and always again,
working, working, working...

to have the peace of mind to go back up into my mountains.

this is my journey.
